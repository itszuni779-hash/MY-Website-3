SAFE ORDERING CHECKLIST

  • Confirm exact model, memory size, brand, new/used condition and stock before payment.
  • Ask for a dated product photo or video and a written final price.
  • Use a payment method with buyer protection where possible.
  • Never share passwords, OTPs, card PINs or recovery codes.
  • Keep receipts, tracking details and all order messages.
